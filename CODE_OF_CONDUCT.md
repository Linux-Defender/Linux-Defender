# Code of Conduct

Linux Defender follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html). We welcome people of all backgrounds and expect empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive feedback, accountability, and attention to the community's good.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, trolling, derogatory attacks, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not include private details in a public issue. Maintainers should review reports promptly and fairly while protecting reporter privacy. Enforcement may include a correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban depending on severity and pattern.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant. Its Community Impact Guidelines are inspired by Mozilla's enforcement ladder; see the Covenant's [FAQ](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/faq/) and [translations](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/translations/).
